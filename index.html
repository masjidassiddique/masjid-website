WHO WE ARE

More than a masjid.
A centre for growth.

Masjid-as-Siddique (RA) Complex strives to build a spiritually strong, educated, and connected Muslim community. Through programs for men, women, youth, and children, we work to spread the authentic teachings of Islam and revive the masjid’s role as a centre of learning, unity, and community development.

Inspired by the time of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, when the masjid was the heart of worship, education, and society, we aim to make our masjid a welcoming hub for the whole community.
